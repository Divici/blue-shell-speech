Why this screen is empty

Patient records, your schedule and clinical notes are fetched from the server each time you open them, and are never stored on this device. That is deliberate — a phone that keeps a copy of a child’s record is a phone that can lose one. It also means there is nothing here for this screen to show you.

What needs a connection

  • Your schedule and today’s visits
  • Every patient record, guardian and goal
  • Opening, editing or signing a clinical note

What to do

  1. Move somewhere with signal — a doorway or the street usually beats the middle of a house.
  2. Then try again. The app will load normally as soon as it can reach the server.
